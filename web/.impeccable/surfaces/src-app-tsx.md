# Surface: Anchor Studio app (all signed-in pages) + landing

Scope: the whole web app (Desk, Script, Visuals, Review, Render queue, Library, Anchors, Settings) in Operate mode; the landing page and sign-in in Persuade mode, same world. Users: DNL desk editors at a desktop, several stories a day. Content: real seed data (public/seed). Constraint: dark (user-pinned), DNL red mark, backend not connected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every story is a roll of film. Picking pictures is marking the contact sheet; timing is the edge print. Refuses the category default of a grey NLE clone with one blue accent.

OWN-WORLD: black film-rebate ground (#0a0b0d; the contact sheet itself #060607) and an eleven-step graphite ramp for every neutral; amber edge-print mono (frame codes R03·F12, timecodes, counts); DNL china-marker red for selection, primary action and on-air; violet (#ab9cff) for anchor parts, steel blue (#5fb3ea) for voiceover; hairline seams, 2px radii; one workhorse sans (Archivo, width axis for labels) plus JetBrains Mono for edge print; marker numerals only on picked frames.

STORY: the editor sees what needs them, writes and times the script against the target, marks frames per section until every section is full, hears the whole voice over a rough cut, and sends it to the queue, then starts the next story while rings fill.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Visuals): top bar with DNL mark, story title, step track; left rail of sections whose heights are proportional to seconds with fill bars; centre contact sheet of frames with amber codes and fit scores, selected frames looped in red with play-order numerals; right picked list in play order with seconds and a locked Continue naming what is missing.

FORM: Contact Sheet, position 5 of 7 on my ordered list; seed:b504cf6f601f2f88. Raises: proportional section rail (acetate manual); stable frame codes everywhere (catalog sleeve); one 11-step neutral ramp (exposure record); next moment preloaded in the viewer (vertical feed); every locked gate names what is ahead (terminal wayfinding).

SIGNATURE: the china-marker select: picking a frame draws a red loop around it (stroke animation, 220 ms) and writes its play-order numeral; reordering rewrites the numerals.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
